← Open ClipForge

Your familiar studio. Now on the web.

1. Start with a video

Paste your YouTube or direct video link and click Import video, or drop an original recording into the upload area. Replicate downloads and prepares it. Browser uploads support 500 MB; video links support files up to 2 GB and four hours. Some websites block cloud downloads; use your original recording if that happens.

2. Give every project a direction

Use Project folders → ＋ to create a home for a channel or series. Add a prompt explaining the audience, tone and ideas you want. Add a YouTube channel link to browse its recent videos. In AI & settings, describe yourself and your website. Empty fields stay empty—there is no built-in promotion for somebody else's business.

3. Make the cut

Choose clip length, quantity and transcription accuracy, then Generate clips. CPU transcription runs on Replicate. OpenRouter is optional for AI editorial suggestions and uses your own key. Select words to seek, drag the timeline to focus on a section, or add a manual clip. Use Edit words to correct captions.

4. Make it yours

Open Edit clip for start/end times, vertical or horizontal framing, focus, zoom keyframes, caption colors and quality. Click Save & render to see the final result. Download MP4s or the ZIP, or keep clips in Saved.

5. Put your posts on the calendar

Need a home for your social accounts? Explore Aspire Builder Pro ↗ and create an account. Its Social Planner can bring your publishing into one place. This is optional; an existing compatible GoHighLevel account works too.

  1. In your account, connect the social profiles you want to publish to using Social Planner.
  2. Open account settings and find Private Integrations. Create a token with social planner account/read, post/read, post/write, media/write and users/read permissions.
  3. Copy your location ID and integration token into ClipForge's AI & settings.
  4. Click Test connection, choose who is posting, and save.
  5. Use Schedule for one clip or Quick schedule for a reviewed lineup. Pick the title/caption and first comment for each post, then schedule the reviewed posts.

Each provider may require its own account plan or permissions. Never post your tokens in chat or public files.

For the website owner

The PHP host must serve only the public directory and keep private outside the web root. Install the included background worker as a cron job so files are saved before Replicate links expire. Read the full hosting guide ↗.
